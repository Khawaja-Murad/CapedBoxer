Publications

The Diagnosis a Reporter Leaves Unspoken: Surfacing Frozen Tumor Features for Brain-Tumor MRI Reporting Accepted
K. M. U. Hassan · MICCAI MLCN Workshop 2026 · presenting in person, Strasbourg, 1 Oct 2026
Identifies a "diagnostic suppression" effect in brain-tumor MRI reporting models: the correct diagnosis is linearly recoverable from frozen segmentation features (0.82 macro-F1) but goes unspoken by the reporter. NeuroFusion surfaces it via discriminative-head conditioning, restoring diagnosis recall (e.g. meningioma 0.44→0.92) and beating four external baselines. In a blinded two-neurologist reader study it was rated highest across every tumor type with zero critical errors. Deployed in production in AI Sehat Bridge.
